Imintty is the [Cygwin](http://cygwin.com) Terminal, 
as well as the 
[terminal emulator](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_emulator) 
for MSYS 
([Msys2](https://www.msys2.org),
[old MSYS](https://web.archive.org/web/20201223203407/http://www.mingw.org/)).

For an introduction, features overview, and screenshots, see the 
[<img align=absmiddle src=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/imintty/imintty/refs/heads/master/icon/terminal.ico>Imintty homepage](http://imintty.github.io/).
